06 / Straight answers

Before you
open another tab.

Common questions about spreadsheets, shopping agents, product checks, and delivery costs.

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a curated list of product links used to organize shopping research. Entries may include categories, seller information, prices, sizes, and notes. A listing is a starting point for checking a product, not proof of quality or authenticity.

Is this page a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page is a category-based shopping guide that links to CAFANS collections. It does not provide a downloadable spreadsheet, live stock feed, or verified Kakobuy integration.

How do I use product links with a shopping agent?

Find the original seller link on the product listing, then check whether your chosen agent accepts that link and item. Confirm the available options, service terms, and destination restrictions before paying. Compatibility is not guaranteed.

What should I check in QC photos?

Check the selected color and size, visible construction, stitching, measurements, included accessories, and any damage. Request additional photos when needed. QC photos do not independently establish authenticity.

How much will shipping cost?

Shipping depends on the destination, carrier, parcel dimensions, chargeable weight, and service selected. Include applicable handling fees, taxes, and duties when estimating the total. Obtain a current quote before committing.

Are the featured brands affiliated with this guide?

No brand affiliation or endorsement is claimed. Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci, and Hermès are referenced for editorial context. The graphics are conceptual illustrations, not product photographs or evidence of stock, authenticity, or authorization.
